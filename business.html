AI for business

At work I build AI systems for companies and teach the people who are going to use them. Here I write about processes: which steps are good for AI, which ones want a plain program, and which ones stay with a person.

You don’t need to know how to code to read this part.

Start with your own process

Think of something your team repeats every week and write its steps. Mark each one, and see where AI could go.

RRead. Reading, summarizing, classifying or drafting.
CCalculate. Math, or a rule that is already written.
DDecide. Something that somebody answers for.
